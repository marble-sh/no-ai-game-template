# IDEAS — [Your Game]

The running box for every game idea. Maintained by the project manager (the AI
assistant). Nothing here is a promise: ideas are built only when **accepted**, and
an accepted idea is broken into board issues at that moment — blockers, `ready`
labels, priorities and due dates included.

**Feasibility (F1–F5)** — how hard it would be *for you, right now*:

- **F5** comfortable — mostly C/raylib you already know
- **F4** doable — well-trodden path; docs and examples will carry you
- **F3** a real stretch — new technique; expect a learning detour
- **F2** hard — new paradigm (UI systems, serialization, runtime behaviour)
- **F1** research-y — would become a milestone of its own

**Points** — total estimated work, anchored to your real schedule:

1 ≈ under one evening · 2 ≈ one evening · 3 ≈ a weekend block · 5 ≈ 2–3 evenings ·
8 ≈ about a week of sessions · 13 = epic — must be split before it starts.

**Status** — `proposed` → `accepted` (issues exist) → `shipped` · also `parked` / `rejected`.
